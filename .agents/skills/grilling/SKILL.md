# Grilling

Interview for the current scope. For a new project, settle the first usable MVP;
record later capabilities as exclusions or open decisions rather than designing
the whole future system.

1. Establish users, the current workflow/problem, observable outcomes, scope,
   sources of truth, and the critical constraints. Inspect only named docs or
   dependencies; research facts from primary sources instead of asking the user
   to look them up. Small lookups stay local; delegate only when useful.
2. Ask at most three independent, decision-ready questions per round. Give each
   a recommended answer and its trade-off; wait for the user's answers. Ask a
   dependent question only after its prerequisite is settled. Business decisions
   belong to the user; separate researched facts from proposals and assumptions.
3. Test settled rules with concrete failures or boundary scenarios, especially
   ownership, money, dates, external failures, retries, and concurrent changes
   when applicable. Focus on what changes the MVP's behavior or feasibility.
4. Summarize scope and decisions for the user to confirm. Keep unresolved choices
   with their owner and affected work; other work may proceed when independent.
   Finish with the agreed MVP and its remaining blockers, ready for a PRD.
